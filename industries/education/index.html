Everything Your School Needs to Manage Families, From Open Day to Re-Enrollment

The demo follows Hillside, a fictional private school with three campuses, from preschool to a bachelor's program: admissions, families, students and re-enrollment. Every screen can be adapted to the way your school works during a Setup Week, and new ones added for the rest of your school life.

A Dashboard for Admissions and Re-Enrollment

Start the day with this month's inquiries, applications submitted, offers pending and enrolled students against seats. The funnel and the seats per program follow the intake, program, campus and source you pick, and each stage links to the filtered list behind it. Offers awaiting an answer and re-enrollment progress sit right next to them.

Record an Inquiry in One Form

A parent calls or stops by at the open day: one form creates the family, the guardian, the child, the application at the first stage, a note and the consents. If the family already exists, the CRM says so and attaches the new child to it.

Families and Guardians

The family page shows the household, the children with their age and current school, and every application. Each guardian has clickable roles (parental authority, payer, emergency contact, pickup authorized), and consents are recorded per channel with their date and source.

An Admissions Pipeline on a Kanban Board

Inquiry, Visit, Application, Offer, Enrolled: drag the cards from one stage to the next. Each card shows the child's age at intake, the program, a sibling badge, the documents counter and the answer deadline. Switch to the list view for bulk actions: change the stage, assign a counselor or export to CSV.

Required Documents and Admission Decisions

Decide which documents each program requires: birth certificate, school reports, vaccination record, ID photo. On the application page, one click marks a document received or validated. Moving a card to the Offer stage asks for the decision (admitted, waitlisted, refused) and sets the date the family must answer by.

Re-Enrollment Campaigns

Every spring, create next year's applications for all enrolled students in one step, with the next grade suggested. Running the campaign twice never creates duplicates. Each family's intention (confirmed, undecided, leaving) is one click away, and families at risk stand out.

A Good-To-Know Card on Every Child

Allergies, medical conditions, diet, custody restrictions and other instructions live on the student's page, next to who may pick the child up and who to call in an emergency. A yearly check reminds staff to confirm the information with the family, and alert pills follow the child on every list and card.

Building Blocks, Not Modules

A preschool, a K-12 school and a business school don't need different education software, just different settings. Programs with their seats, fees and entry age, campuses, intakes, sources, required documents, admission stages and application types are lists you edit in Settings. The student is not a separate module: a child is a person, linked to guardians with roles, from the first inquiry to the last school year.

School and Education CRM: Frequently Asked Questions

What is an education CRM, and why choose an open-source one?

An education CRM, also called a school CRM, admissions software or enrollment management software, keeps track of the families and students around your school and everything you do with them, from the first inquiry to the last school year: visits, applications, documents, decisions, good-to-know information and re-enrollment. Most of these tools charge per user, per student or per module. Atomic CRM is open source: no per-seat fee, and code you can adapt and extend to the way your school works.

Is Atomic CRM really free for a school?

Atomic CRM itself is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no per-user fee. Running it for your school means hosting it and adapting the education template to the way you work, with your own developer or with our team. If you'd rather not deal with servers, we can also host it for you, with your data in Europe or the US.

Can I use it for my school today?

Not as is. The demo shows the full admissions and re-enrollment workflow but runs entirely in your browser, and its data resets on every reload. Going live means connecting these screens to a database on Supabase or your own infrastructure and adapting the settings to your school. That is what the Setup Week is for.

Does it replace our student information system or school management software?

No. Atomic CRM manages the relationship with families: inquiries, applications, documents, decisions, the good-to-know card on every student and re-enrollment. Timetables, grades and attendance stay in your student information system, such as PowerSchool or Veracross. Syncing the two is an extension you can build.

Can we import our families from Finalsite, Blackbaud or a spreadsheet?

Yes. Export your families, students and applicants as a CSV file from your current admissions software or spreadsheet, then import them with column mapping and merge the duplicates. Bringing over the full history of past seasons usually takes a custom import script, written by your developer or by our team.

Is it only for admissions?

No. Admissions are where the demo starts, but families, guardians and students stay in the CRM for their whole time at your school: the family page, the good-to-know card and the yearly re-enrollment are already in the demo. Family communication, parent-teacher meetings, a family portal, fees or alumni relations are extensions you can build on the same data.

Does it work for preschools, K-12 schools and higher education?

Yes. Programs, intakes, stages, required documents and application types are settings. A preschool tracks families and a toddler's nap; a business school tracks student recruitment by program and intake. The same screens work for both without changing code.

How does it handle children's personal and medical data?

Consent is recorded per channel (email, SMS, phone, photo) with its date and source. The good-to-know card keeps allergies, medical needs and custody instructions with a yearly check, and alert pills show only the category and a short summary. Restricting the medical details to the school nurse and setting retention rules are part of a Setup Week. You choose where your data is hosted: on your own servers, or by us in Europe.

Can families apply online?

Not in the demo: staff record inquiries with a single form. A public inquiry form on your website and a family portal to upload documents are extensions to build, with our team or your own developer.

Does it collect application fees and deposits?

Not by itself. The application shows the tuition fee of the program; online payments, deposits and scholarships are an extension to build, usually by syncing a payment platform such as Stripe.
