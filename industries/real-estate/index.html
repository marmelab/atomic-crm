All the Essentials to Close Deals Faster

Everything your team needs to manage the full lifecycle of a real estate transaction — from first meeting to final signature.

Smart Contact Hub

Keep every detail about your clients — email, phone, LinkedIn, pronouns, background notes, and custom tags — organised in one searchable profile. No more scattered spreadsheets.

Deal Pipeline Management

Visualise every deal on a drag-and-drop Kanban board. Move opportunities from first contact to signed contract and see your pipeline value update in real time.

Company Directory

Track the brokerages, developers, and vendors your team works with. Filter by sector, size, or active deals — and see which contacts belong to each organisation.

Detailed Contact Editing

Capture identity, position, personal info, phone numbers, LinkedIn, background context, and assign account managers — all from a clean, structured edit form.
