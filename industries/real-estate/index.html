Why Choose Atomic CRM?

Agency software is often sold per agent and per module, with paid portal gateways, long contracts and painful migrations. Agents end up typing the same data into three tools. Atomic CRM is a toolkit you own: start from a working real estate CRM, and shape it to the way your agency works.

No Per-Agent Fees, No Lock-In

Open source and self-hosted. Add agents, assistants and interns without a new licence, and export all your data at any time.

Every Lead Answered

Portal enquiries land in one inbox, linked to the right property, checked for duplicates and assigned to an agent automatically. The time since reception is always visible.

The Right Buyer for Each Property

Buyer search criteria are matched against your stock in both directions, with a score that explains why a property fits or misses.

Sellers Kept in the Loop

Visits, feedback and offers are recorded as they happen, so you can tell an owner exactly where their sale stands.

No Missed Deadline

Mandate expiry, cooling-off period, loan condition and deed date are tracked for you, with reminders before it is too late.

Your Agency at a Glance

Active mandates, exclusive share, visits, pending offers and fees signed or collected on one screen, for the whole team or one agent.

Everything Your Agency Needs, From Mandate to Final Deed

A complete agency workflow out of the box: the property file, the mandate, the buyer, the visit, the offer and the deal. Every screen is plain React code you can change.

Properties Your Clients Can Picture

Browse your stock as a grid of photo cards and filter it by status, type, sale or rental, city, price range or agent. Each property page gathers photos, surfaces, rooms, energy rating, the owner (a person or an organization such as an SCI) and the full price history, so every price drop is on record.

Mandates and a Register That Cannot Be Tampered With

Create a simple, exclusive or semi-exclusive mandate from the property page. The register number is assigned automatically and can never be edited, the register exports to CSV, and a renewal task is created 30 days before the mandate expires.

One Inbox for Portal Leads

Enquiries from listing portals and your website are parsed into a name, an email, a phone number and the listing they refer to. One click turns a lead into a contact, or merges it with an existing one when the email or phone already exists. New leads are assigned to the property's agent or in rotation across the team.

Buyer Matching That Explains Itself

Record what each buyer is looking for: budget, cities, property types, surface, rooms, energy rating. The property page lists matching buyers, the buyer page lists matching properties, and each match shows which criteria fit and which do not. Plan a visit straight from the list.

Visits With a Report in Ten Seconds

Schedule visits from the property or from a matching buyer. Afterwards, the agent picks Liked, Mixed or Not interested and adds a comment. Feedback adds up on the property page, ready for the next call with the owner.

Offers, Counter-Offers and a Clear Sales Pipeline

Every offer and counter-offer is timestamped on the deal and never deleted. Accepting an offer moves the deal forward and puts the property under offer. The Kanban board has five readable stages, from offer to sold or lost, and each card shows the next deadline: end of the cooling-off period, loan condition, final deed. The deal page shows the whole journey as a timeline, with the notary and the mortgage broker in charge.

Your Agency Dashboard

KPI tiles for mandates, exclusives, visits, pending offers and fees, a monthly fees chart, the active buyer searches and a Needs attention card that lists what to do today: expiring mandates, unanswered leads, close deadlines, properties without a visit for 30 days and visits waiting for a report. Filter it all by agent.

MCP Server and AI Agents

Talk to Your CRM

Agents spend their days on the road, between two visits. With the built-in MCP server, they can ask Claude, ChatGPT or any MCP-compatible assistant about their properties, buyers and deals in plain language, and update the CRM without opening it.

Ask Your Pipeline
“Which exclusive mandates expire this month, and which of those properties had no visit in the last 30 days?” “What are the pending offers on the Lyon apartments, and the best one for each?”
Find the Right Buyers
“A 3-room apartment in Annecy just came in at €420,000. Which active buyers match it? Draft a short email for each of them.”
Report From the Car
“Log the visit of 2 rooms open view in Nantes with Janis Kohler: she liked it, worried about the noise, wants a second visit on Saturday.” The assistant records the report and creates the follow-up task.
Start the Day Prepared
“List my visits and follow-ups for today with the owner's phone number and the last feedback on each property.”
Your Data, Your Rules
The assistant goes through the same API and permissions as the app. Nothing is copied to a third-party CRM, and you choose which assistant to connect.

What You Can Build Next

Because Atomic CRM is a toolkit and not a closed product, your team or your agency can add what your market needs, on top of the same data model.

Listing Syndication

Publish properties to portals through a standard feed or a syndication service, check mandatory mentions (fees, energy rating) before going live, and track leads and cost per portal.

Owner Reports and Portal

A weekly report generated from visits and feedback, sent in one click, or a read-only owner page with a magic link.

E-Signature and Documents

Send mandates, visit vouchers and offers for signature through Yousign or DocuSign, with a document checklist per deal stage and a shared file for the notary and the broker.

Valuations and Prospecting

Comparable sales from open data (DVF in France) on the property page, valuation reports in PDF, and a call queue for owners who sell on their own.

Lettings and Light Property Management

Online tenant applications with document checks, lease generation, check-in and check-out reports with photos, rent receipts and indexation.

Alerts and Messaging

Email alerts to buyers when a matching property or a price drop appears, SMS visit confirmations, and a unified inbox for email, SMS and WhatsApp.

Compliance

Anti-money-laundering checks (client identification, risk level, register ready for an audit) and tracking of professional cards and mandatory training.

Commissions and Invoicing

Fee splits between agents and agencies, targets per agent, invoices and reconciliation with the payments received from the notary.

And with the MCP server and the AI builder described above, many of these can start as a prompt.

Not Just Residential Agencies

Real estate knowledge (property types and statuses, mandate kinds, deal stages and deadlines, partner types, French rules such as the mandate register and the 10-day cooling-off period) lives in one swappable configuration pack, editable in the Settings page. The rest is a generic core for any business that sells or rents assets to clients: commercial real estate brokers, new-build developers and property managers, and beyond real estate, car dealers or yacht brokers.
