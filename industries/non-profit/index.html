Everything Your Association Needs, From Registration to the Last Match

The demo follows one season of Riverside FC, a neighbourhood football club. It covers the full workflow out of the box, and every screen is plain React code you can change.

A Dashboard for the Whole Club

Start the week with the next 7 days of events and how many people are expected, fees collected versus outstanding, missing and expiring documents, members per team, the grants pipeline and tasks due. Each block links to the filtered list behind it.

Members and Their Families

Register a child and add a parent in the same flow. Each person shows their age and a "minor" badge, their guardians, groups, memberships and documents. Consent is recorded per channel (email, SMS, post) with its date, source and legal basis, and custom fields (shirt size, licence number…) are defined in Settings, without code.

Teams, Crews and Committees

Groups have a type, a season and members with a role and dates: players and coaches in a team, volunteers in the snack-bar crew, the board. The group page shows the roster with each member's fee status and missing documents.

Membership Fees, Even in Installments

Plans are priced per period (season, calendar year or date to date). Each membership has a payment schedule, a balance and a status (up to date, overdue, expired). Record a payment in two clicks; overdue members get a reminder addressed to their guardian.

Money In, Money Out

Every payment becomes a transaction, linked to a person, a membership or an organization, with configurable categories (fees, sponsorship, grant, equipment, snack bar) and totals that follow your filters.

Required Documents With Expiry Alerts

Decide which documents each plan or group requires: medical certificate, parental authorization, photo, volunteer charter. The CRM tracks validity dates, lists what is missing, expired or about to expire, and reminds the legal guardian. Medical documents are visible to administrators only.

Events, Recurring Trainings and Attendance

Create "every Wednesday at 6 pm from September to June" in one form, skip school holidays, invite a whole team, and ask for helpers (snack bar, referee). Coaches take attendance on their phone; each person's attendance rate builds up over the season.

Messages to a Team or a Segment

Email the parents of the U11s, the volunteers, or everyone with an unpaid fee. Minors are replaced by their guardians, people who have not given email consent are left out, you see the final recipient list before sending, and each message is logged in every recipient's history.

Sponsors and Grants Pipeline

Follow local sponsors and the city-hall grant on a drag-and-drop board, from first contact to payment, with tasks for every deadline.

Building Blocks, Not Modules

A football club, a coworking space and a clothing bank don't need different software, just different settings. Group types, member roles, event types, transaction categories, document types and relationship types are lists you edit in Settings, like the stages of a pipeline. Even the vocabulary is yours: call people "Members", "Residents" or "Beneficiaries". A volunteer is not a separate module, just a person in a group with a role on events.

What You Can Add to Your Association CRM

Because Atomic CRM is a toolkit and not a closed product, your team or your agency can add what your association needs, on top of the same data model.

Payment Platform Sync

A HelloAsso, Stripe, GoCardless or PayPal payment creates the person, the membership and the transaction automatically.

Tax Receipts and Gift Aid

Numbered donation receipts (Cerfa 11580 in France, yearly receipts) and country packs such as UK Gift Aid.

Campaigns and Major Gifts

Fundraising goals compared to amounts raised, and separate pipelines for major gifts, legacies and corporate sponsorship.

Season Rollover

Renew groups for the new season and move players up an age category in one step.

Federation Licences

Import licence files from your federation's software instead of typing them again.

Bookable Resources and Online Sign-Up

Rooms, pitches and vehicles, plus event registration with capacity limits for workshops and volunteer shifts.

Newsletter Sync and Mail Merge

Sync segments and unsubscribes with Brevo or Mailchimp, print letters and address labels.

Volunteer Hours and Impact Reports

Hours derived from attendance and valued, member retention, beneficiaries served, and the annual report.

Roles and Board Handover

Treasurer, board, coach limited to their teams, and accounts tied to a role rather than a person.

Member Portal and General Assembly

Members update their details, renew and download receipts; general assemblies with proxies, quorum and online votes.

Non-Profit CRM: Frequently Asked Questions

What is a non-profit CRM, and why choose an open-source one?

A non-profit CRM keeps track of the people around your organization (members, families, volunteers, donors, partners) and everything you do with them: memberships, payments, documents, events and messages. Most non-profit software charges per user or per contact, which hurts organizations run by volunteers. Atomic CRM is open source: no per-seat fee, and code you can adapt to the way your association works.

Is Atomic CRM really free for an association?

The code is open source under the MIT licence, with no per-user fee and no paid module. You pay only for what you choose to run it with: hosting, and the time to set it up, by your own developer or during a Setup Week with our team.

Can I use it for my association today?

Not as is. The demo shows the full workflow but runs entirely in your browser, and its data resets on every reload. Going live means connecting these screens to a database on Supabase or your own infrastructure and adapting the settings to your association. That is what the Setup Week is for.

Does it collect membership fees and donations online?

Not by itself, and that is deliberate: payment platforms like HelloAsso, Stripe or Zeffy already do this well, often for free. In the demo you record payments by hand; syncing a payment platform so that a payment creates the member, the membership and the transaction is an extension to build.

How does it handle minors and their parents?

Each child is linked to their parents, with a legal guardian flag. Fee and document reminders, as well as messages to a team, go to the legal guardians automatically, and a minor's page shows who to call in an emergency.

How does it handle GDPR and medical documents?

Consent is recorded per channel (email, SMS, post) with its date, source and legal basis, and messages skip people who have not consented. Documents flagged as sensitive, such as medical certificates, are visible to administrators only; other users only see whether the document is valid. Self-hosting keeps all data on infrastructure you control.

Is it only for sports clubs?

No. The football club is the demo's example. Group types, roles, plans, event types, documents and even the vocabulary are settings, so the same screens work for a coworking space, a clothing bank or a music school without changing code.

Does it replace our accounting software?

No. Atomic CRM records who paid what and when, linked to people and memberships, but it does not do double-entry accounting. Exporting transactions to your accounting tool is an extension you can build.
