A Full-Featured Non-Profit CRM

Atomic CRM offers the key features for your mission-driven team.

Capture Mission-Critical Insights

Log notes after every call or event, tag key supporters, and schedule follow-ups with due dates, all from a single profile. Never miss a donor thank-you or a volunteer check-in again.

Track Funding Pipelines

Visualize every grant and donation on a drag-and-drop Kanban board. Move opportunities from first proposal to secured funding and see your pipeline value update in real time.

Organize Supporter Profiles

Keep track of the foundations, agencies, and partner organizations you work with. Filter by sector or size and see which contacts belong to each organization.

Detailed Supporter Records

Capture pronouns, role, organization, contact details, how you met, and newsletter preferences, and assign a relationship manager to every supporter from one structured form.

Built for How Non-Profits Really Work

For teams that are part volunteer, run on tight budgets, and handle sensitive data.

Ensure Seamless Data Migration & Integration

Import donor databases, volunteer rosters, and grant records from spreadsheets or legacy systems with zero hassle.

Track Activity History

Get a comprehensive view of all supporter interactions, including emails, tasks, new donations and contacts, with aggregated activity logs.

Unify your Volunteer Management

Manage your workforce without the “user tax”. Since Atomic CRM has no per-seat fees, you can give every temporary volunteer a login for task tracking without breaking the budget.

Ensure Data Sovereignty

For organizations handling sensitive beneficiary or patient data, Atomic CRM can be self-hosted on your own Supabase instance, ensuring 100% compliance and privacy.

Improved Workflow With Advanced Capabilities

Exclusive features designed to improve productivity and collaboration.

Single Sign-On (SSO)

Log in using your organizational accounts, including Google, Azure, Keycloak, and Auth0.

Track Funding Pipelines

Visualize and manage your fundraising and grant pipelines with customizable stages and a drag-and-drop card system.

Email to Note

CC Atomic CRM on emails to automatically save communications as notes: from donor thank-yous, volunteer updates, to grant communications, no manual entry, no lost context.

Contact Avatars

Enrich contact details by letting the CRM automatically pull avatars from the web using their email.

API Integration

Tap into Atomic CRM's API to effortlessly integrate with other systems, providing a cohesive and unified data environment.

MCP Server

Connect your favorite AI assistant and talk to your CRM in plain language—query, update, and explore your data naturally.
