The Open-Source CRM for Hair Salons: Keep Every Client Coming Back

Bookings, color formulas, reminders and rebooking in one tool your salon owns. No marketplace commission on your own clients, no per-chair fees, and full control over your data.

Why Choose Atomic CRM?

Booking marketplaces charge salons up to 30% on new clients and keep the client relationship for themselves. Atomic CRM is a toolkit you own: start from a working salon CRM, and shape it to the way your salon works.

No Commission, No Lock-In

Your clients book on your own page. Open source, self-hosted, and no fee on the clients you already have.

Fewer No-Shows

Automatic reminders the day before and two hours before, with one-tap confirm or cancel, and a cancellation policy your front desk can enforce.

Formulas That Never Get Lost

Color formulas, processing times and products are saved for every visit, so they no longer live in a notebook or leave with a stylist.

Built for the Chair

A mobile mode with large type, the last formula and allergies first, and before/after photos taken right from the phone.

Clients Who Come Back

Rebooking suggested at checkout and recalls when a client's usual cycle has passed. Retention becomes routine.

Your Salon at a Glance

Today's appointments, free slots, takings, occupancy, no-show and rebooking rates on one screen.

Everything Your Salon Needs, From Booking to Rebooking

A complete salon workflow out of the box: the agenda, the client file, the front desk and the follow-up. Every screen is plain React code you can change.

Multi-Stylist Agenda

See the whole team's day in one view, with one column per stylist. Services are made of work and processing time, so a stylist can start the next client while a color processes. Drag to move or resize an appointment. Absences and off-hours are greyed out, and a week view shows each stylist's schedule.

Book in Three Clicks

Click a free slot, find the client by name or phone (or create one on the spot), and add one or more services. Conflicts with other bookings are flagged before you save.

A Technical File for Every Client

Each visit records a structured formula: technique, brand, shades and proportions, developer, processing time, products. Changes from the previous visit are highlighted, and "Reuse last formula" pre-fills the next one. Allergies and patch tests stay in a red banner at the top of the file, visible to stylists and managers only.

Online Booking on Your Own Page

Clients book in four mobile-friendly steps: services, stylist or "no preference", time slot, then name and phone with a verification code. No account to create, and the cancellation policy is shown before they confirm. Share it with a link or a QR code on Instagram, Google or the shop window.

Reminders and Messages

Reminders the day before and two hours before, review requests after the visit, recalls and birthday messages are generated from editable templates. The confirmation status appears on each appointment. In the demo, every message lands in an outbox so you can see what would be sent.

Today's Dashboard

Start the day with today's appointments, free slots per stylist, takings so far, and the clients due for a recall, each with one-click SMS or booking. Weekly indicators compare revenue, occupancy, no-show and rebooking rates with the previous week.

Services and Team

Build the service catalog with durations, prices, processing segments, eligible stylists and return cycles. Manage each team member's weekly schedule, absences and role (front desk, stylist, manager) from tabs on their profile.

What You Can Build Next

Because Atomic CRM is a toolkit and not a closed product, your team or your agency can add what your salon needs, on top of the same data model.

Deposits and No-Show Protection

Card imprint or deposit for long services or unreliable clients, with a reliability score per client.

Waitlist

Offer freed-up slots automatically to clients who asked to be notified.

Gift Cards, Packages and Loyalty Points

Prepaid bundles (e.g. 10 blow-dries), balances visible at checkout.

Commissions

Rules per service category, product sales and tiers, with a monthly statement per stylist.

Retail and Stock

Product catalog, stock alerts, color consumption per service and supplier orders.

Multi-Location

Shared client base, consolidated reporting, stylists working across salons.

More Booking Channels

Reserve with Google, WhatsApp booking, or a phone assistant using the built-in MCP server.

Real Payments and Certified Checkout

Connect a payment terminal and a certified POS provider where required (for example NF525 in France).

Not Just Hair Salons

Hair-specific knowledge (formula fields, service categories, return cycles, message templates) lives in one swappable configuration pack. The rest is a generic core for any appointment-based business: barbers, beauty and nail salons, spas, physiotherapists, pet groomers or tattoo studios.
