title: Deals
---

Deals track your sales opportunities. They are displayed as cards in a Kanban board, one column per stage of your pipeline, and you move a deal to another stage by dragging its card.

## Deal Categories

A deal can belong to several categories. For instance, a single deal may include a Sprint 0, a development sprint and a license. Pick as many categories as needed in the **Categories** field of the deal form; the deal budget remains a single amount for the whole deal.

The categories of a deal are displayed on its card in the Kanban board, on the deal page, and in the list of deals of its company.

The available categories are configured by administrators in the [Application Settings](../../users/settings) page. A category can't be removed while deals still use it.

## Finding Deals

- The **Category** filter shows the deals that include the selected category, whatever their other categories.
- The search box matches the name, the description and the categories of a deal. A category is found by its displayed label or by its stored value.

## Importing and Exporting Deals

Deals can be imported from and exported to CSV files, with their categories in a `categories` column separated by `;`. See [Importing and Exporting Data](../../users/import-data).
