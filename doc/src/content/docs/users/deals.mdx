---
title: Deals
---

Deals track your sales opportunities. They are displayed as cards in a Kanban board, one column per stage of your pipeline, and you move a deal to another stage by dragging its card.

## Deal Categories and Budget

A deal can include several categories, each with its own amount. For instance, a single deal may include a Sprint 0, a development sprint and a license. In the deal form, add one line per category with its amount; the deal budget is the sum of these amounts, computed automatically.

The categories of a deal are displayed on its card in the Kanban board and in the list of deals of its company. The deal page shows the amount of each category.

The available categories are configured by administrators in the [Application Settings](../../users/settings) page. A category can't be removed while deals still use it.

## Finding Deals

- The **Category** filter accepts several categories, and shows the deals that include all of them.
- The search box matches the name, the description and the categories of a deal. A category is found by its displayed label or by its stored value.

## Importing and Exporting Deals

Deals can be imported from and exported to CSV files, with their categories and amounts in a `categories` column (`category:amount`, separated by `;`). See [Importing and Exporting Data](../../users/import-data).
